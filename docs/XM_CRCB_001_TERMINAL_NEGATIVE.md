## 2. Intended role of XM-CRCB-001

XM-CRCB-001 was not an authority experiment and did not revive the earlier XM authority-starvation hypothesis.

Its intended role was narrower:

> Test whether a prospectively fixed repair substrate could first support reliable dedicated regional repair and, only if that primitive gate passed, whether repair structure could then be factorized and transferred across held-out contexts.

The direct-repair adequacy gate was deliberately placed before factorization so that a factorization result could not be manufactured on top of a substrate that had never demonstrated adequate dedicated repair.

## 4. Earned scientific statement

Under the frozen XM-CRCB-001 calibration regime, none of the three predeclared parameter-local repair languages produced sufficiently repeatable, target-specific improvement while satisfying the frozen collateral-damage constraints.

Equivalently:

> The preregistered repair substrate family `L1 subset L2 subset L3` failed the frozen adequacy test.

This is an **assay negative**, not a hypothesis negative.

---

## 5. What the assay does not identify

The terminal negative does not localize the generating cause among at least three live explanations:

1. **Representation failure** — the declared repair-language family may not express the required correction.
2. **Optimization / credit-assignment failure** — the language may be expressive enough, but the frozen `J=32` construction procedure may fail to find adequate corrections.
3. **Structural failure** — the chosen regional competence differences may not be locally repairable in the tested form.

XM-CRCB-001 is closed and may not be modified post hoc to discriminate among these explanations.
