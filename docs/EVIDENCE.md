# Evidence Ledger and Claim Boundary

This document records what the corrigible-compression synthesis is allowed to inherit from prior work, and what it is **not** allowed to claim.

The governing rule is:

```text
adjacent demonstrated facts != synthesis demonstrated
```

Corrigible compression remains a **provisional meta-hypothesis constrained by heterogeneous evidence**.

The retrospective pass asked whether prior results could be compressed as losses or preservation of corrective reach **without collapsing their mechanisms**.

## What the synthesis has not demonstrated

The repository must not claim that any of the following are established:

- Corrigible Compression as a universal property of intelligence;
- a universal typed pipeline from discrepancy to revision;
- a scalar corrigibility metric;
- `R(Pi; C, H, B, T)` as a validated general measure;
- `C_reopen(delta)` as an empirically established universal quantity;
- protected freshness as sufficient for corrigibility;
- metaphysical indeterminacy as an engineering requirement;
- source protection as equivalent to channel protection;
- fresh-seed replication of the high-K XM coupling;
- a causal mechanism behind that coupling.

## Research discipline

The standing experimental/reasoning posture is:

```text
freeze
-> exact execution
-> audit
-> narrow result
-> local revision
```

Prediction failure is evidence about the generating process, not permission for unconstrained theory change.

When contradiction appears:

1. generate competing explanations;
2. discriminate using independent evidence;
3. make the minimum sufficient revision;
4. preserve unaffected structure;
5. retest on held-out or fresh evidence;
6. retain provenance and reopenability until repeated predictive success warrants consolidation.

The synthesis earns authority only to the extent that it continues to compress heterogeneous evidence **without deleting the distinctions that made the evidence informative**.
